Who is Taiyo

One developer, the whole way through

I don't pick between design, code and 3D — what interests me is carrying a thing from the first idea to the moment people actually use it. Which is why a single project of mine can hold a bot, a database, a web panel and its own artwork.

Most of my time right now goes to TANO — a Discord bot that grew into an ecosystem of fifteen systems with its own control panel. Alongside that I build websites, put together scenes in Unity and model in Blender.
